# Salesforce Sales Cloud

## Overview
Salesforce Sales Cloud is an enterprise CRM platform known for its extensibility through add-ons and custom-built extensions. It holds a massive share of the CRM market and is used by hundreds of thousands of companies to run sales operations.

## Key Features
- **Einstein AI**: lead and opportunity scoring, forecasting, activity capture, email generation, call transcription and analysis, deal insights, and pipeline monitoring
- **Einstein Copilot**: AI side tab offering chat experience with suggestions, action plans, and task automation; fully configurable with prompt, skills, and model builder
- **Einstein Trust Layer**: privacy, security, trust, and observability features
- **Agentforce**: AI agent builder grounded on your data, capable of taking action across internal and external systems
- **MCP server**: integrates the Salesforce platform with AI chatbots
- **Data Cloud**: digital infrastructure to host sales data alongside other business data
- **Tableau integration**: generate detailed reports from CRM data
- **Starter Suite**: basic CRM features with email templates and nurture sequences
- **Marketing and customer service features**: track signals as leads interact with pages and sales reps
- **Data field labels**: guide newer sales reps through deals (e.g., discount approval workflows)

## Pricing
From $25/user/month.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS), with mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Integrations
- Zapier integration for workflow automation
- AI assistant access to CRM data
- Tableau for reporting
