## Overview

Google Sheets is a free online spreadsheet application that can be adapted as a lightweight CRM for tracking contacts, leads, and sales pipelines. It requires manual setup and configuration but offers flexibility for custom CRM structures.

## Features

- Free forever with no contact or record limits
- Real-time collaboration with multiple users
- Customizable columns and data validation
- Filtering, sorting, and conditional formatting
- CRM templates available for lead and contact tracking
- Integration with Google Workspace ecosystem
- Zapier integration for connecting to other CRMs and tools

## Pricing

- Free for personal use with a Google account
- Google Workspace paid plans start at $6/user/month for business features

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS with web, Android, and iOS applications.
