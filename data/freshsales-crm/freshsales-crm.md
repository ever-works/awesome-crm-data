# Freshsales CRM

## Overview
Freshsales is a full-fledged CRM designed for high-velocity sales teams. It helps sales teams stay organized, focus on the right deals, and is easy enough that salespeople adopt it readily, making it well suited to small teams.

## Key Features
- Ease of use and intuitive interface for sales teams
- Pipeline and deal management
- Customization options to fit sales processes
- Integration capabilities with other business tools
- Sales automation to reduce manual work

## Pricing
Paid with a free trial available.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).

## Integrations
- Freshworks suite
- Third-party business tool integrations
- API available
