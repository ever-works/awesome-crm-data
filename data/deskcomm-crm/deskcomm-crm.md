## Overview

Deskcomm CRM is an open-source sales CRM designed for WhatsApp. It uses AI agents to answer and qualify leads automatically.

## Features

- WhatsApp integration
- AI agents for lead qualification
- Automated responses
- Lead management
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/melgarafael/DeskcommCRM
