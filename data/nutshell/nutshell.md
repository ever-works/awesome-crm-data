# Nutshell

## Overview
Nutshell is a CRM platform focused on sales team management. It offers three core management tools: territory-based management, deep reporting with forecasts, and customizable notifications to help managers maximize team efficiency.

## Key Features
- **Territory-based management**: automatically assign leads based on postal code, state, and country selectors
- **Deep reporting**: stock reports for main pipeline metrics, reporting by territory, team member, or source
- **Forecasts**: see how present performance may influence future results
- **Customizable notifications**: turn on/off notifications for nearly anything; app-only or email delivery
- **Unlimited contacts**: no contact limits
- **Social media profile links**: grabs social media profile links of contacts
- **Drip email campaigns**: naturally raise interest in products and services
- **Website visitor tracking**: connect to website to follow prospects as they browse
- **AI summarization**: summarizes people, companies, and leads timelines into paragraph overviews
- **Zoom call transcription**: calls transcribed, saved, and summarized
- **Voice-to-text notetaker**: save time from typing

## Pricing
From $19/user/month for unlimited contacts.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS), with mobile apps for iOS and Android.
