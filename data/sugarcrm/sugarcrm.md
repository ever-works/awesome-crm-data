## Overview

SugarCRM is a customer relationship management platform first released in 2004. It provides sales automation, marketing automation, and customer support functionality for businesses of various sizes.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Marketing automation
- Customer support and case management
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (over 40 languages)
- Office integration (Outlook, Excel, Word Plugins)
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking
- LDAP support
- Cross-platform deployment

## Pricing

Proprietary or SaaS licensing model. Various editions available with pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cross-platform deployment; cloud (SaaS) or on-premise options.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel, Word)
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
- Various third-party integrations

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
