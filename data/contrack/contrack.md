## Overview

Contrack is an open-source contact manager designed for personal relationship management. It helps users import, deduplicate, and enrich contacts while providing follow-up reminders and private address books for each family or team member.

## Features

- Contact import and deduplication
- Contact enrichment
- Follow-up reminders
- Private address books for family or team members
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the MIT license.
