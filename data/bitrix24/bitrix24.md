## Overview

Bitrix24 is a comprehensive all-in-one business platform that combines CRM, project management, collaboration, and communication tools in a single system.

## Key Features

- **CRM**: Sales pipeline, contact management, deal tracking, Kanban, list, calendar, and activity views
- **Marketing**: Email marketing, SMS, landing pages, web forms
- **Customer Service**: Contact center, telephony, help desk
- **Project Management**: Tasks, Gantt charts, Kanban boards, time tracking
- **Collaboration**: Group chats, video calls, document management, employee directory
- **HR**: Employee management, time off, company structure
- **E-commerce**: Online store builder, payment processing
- **Website Builder**: Drag-and-drop website and landing page creation
- **eSign**: Electronic signature capabilities
- **Automation**: Pipeline automation, business process automation
- **AI Assistant**: Built-in AI features
- **Integrations**: Native integrations plus Zapier connectivity

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: Unlimited users and contacts, 5GB data storage, basic CRM features
- **Basic Plan**: Starts at $49/month for 5 users
- **Standard, Professional, Enterprise Plans**: Higher tiers with advanced features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) with desktop apps for Windows, Mac, Linux and mobile apps for iOS and Android. Self-hosted option available (Bitrix24 On-Premise).
