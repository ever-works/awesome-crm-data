## Overview

Bigin by Zoho CRM is a pipeline-focused CRM built for small businesses, freelancers, and startups. It simplifies customer relationship management with essential sales tools.

## Key Features

- Pipeline Management
- Contact Management
- Task Management
- Email Integration
- Deal tracking
- Activity reminders

## Pricing

Starts from $0 per user per month, billed annually (free plan). Paid plans available for additional features.

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.

## Integrations

Native integrations with Zoho ecosystem and third-party applications. API-based integrations available.
