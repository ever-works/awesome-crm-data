## Overview

Vtiger is a feature-rich CRM platform designed for sales teams that need strong follow-up automation, combined with marketing, help desk, and project management capabilities.

## Key Features

- **Sales Pipeline**: Visual pipeline management, deal tracking
- **Follow-up Automation**: Email sequences, appointment scheduling, idle deal alerts
- **Email Marketing**: Campaigns, templates, automation
- **Help Desk**: Ticketing, customer support portal
- **Project Management**: Tasks, milestones, project tracking
- **Social Media Integration**: Connect social channels to CRM
- **Calendars**: Appointment booking, availability sharing
- **Notifications**: In-app and email alerts for deal activities
- **Mobile**: iOS and Android apps
- **Integrations**: Native integrations plus Zapier connectivity

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: Up to 10 users, 3,000 records
- **Paid Plans**: Start at $15/user/month
- **Tiered Pricing**: Full-access users and limited-access users at different rates

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) with mobile apps for iOS and Android.
