## Overview

ChurchCRM is an open-source church management CRM for managing people, families, groups, events, attendance, and giving.

## Features

- People management
- Family management
- Group management
- Event management
- Attendance tracking
- Giving/donation tracking
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/ChurchCRM/CRM
