# Insightly

## Overview
Insightly is an easy, powerful, and affordable online Customer Relationship and Project Management software used by over 400,000 small businesses worldwide. It combines CRM capabilities with project management features in one platform.

## Key Features
- User-friendly interface
- Integration capabilities with other business applications
- Customizable to fit business processes
- Project management features alongside CRM
- Affordable pricing for small businesses

## Pricing
Paid with tiered plans.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).

## Integrations
- Third-party business application integrations
- API available
