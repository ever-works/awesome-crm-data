## Overview

Close is a sales CRM platform designed for inside sales teams, first released in 2013. It helps sales teams manage leads, make calls, send emails, and close deals through a unified interface.

## Features

- Lead and contact management
- Built-in calling and SMS
- Email sequences and automation
- Sales pipeline and deal tracking
- Task and activity management
- Web-based interface
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Reporting and analytics

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Email providers
- Calendar systems
- Various sales and marketing tools via API

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
