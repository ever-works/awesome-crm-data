## Overview

OnlyOffice is an open-source office suite that includes CRM functionality, first released in 2010. It provides document editing, spreadsheets, presentations, and customer relationship management capabilities.

## Features

- Document editing and collaboration
- Spreadsheets and presentations
- Customer relationship management
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (en, de, fr, es, it, ru, lat)
- Office integration
- Time tracking
- Email attachment to business objects

## Pricing

GPLv3 open-source edition available free; SaaS and commercial editions available.

## Deployment

Self-hosted on Windows or SaaS/cloud deployment.

## Integrations

- Microsoft SQL Server
- Various cloud storage platforms
- Office document formats
