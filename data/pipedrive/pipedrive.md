## Overview

Pipedrive is a sales CRM platform designed for sales teams, first released in 2011. It provides a visual sales pipeline interface to help teams manage leads, deals, and activities.

## Features

- Visual sales pipeline management
- Lead and contact management
- Deal tracking
- Activity and task management
- Email integration
- Web-based interface
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Multi-language support (de, en, es, et, fi, fr, it, no, nl, pl, pt, ru, tr, ko, ja)
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking
- Desktop environment support

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Office integration
- Email clients
- Various third-party integrations
