## Overview

Adempiere is an open-source enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM) software platform. It was first released in 2006 and is developed by a community of contributors led by Jorg Janke.

## Features

- ERP and CRM functionality
- Multi-language and multi-currency support
- Accounting and financial management
- Supply chain management
- Customer relationship management
- Project management
- Warehouse management
- Cross-platform (Windows, Linux, Unix, Mac)

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPLv2 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted; runs on Windows, Linux, Unix, and Mac.

## Integrations

- Java-based architecture
- Database backend not specified in source
