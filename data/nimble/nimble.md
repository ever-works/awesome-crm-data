# Nimble

## Overview
Nimble is a CRM platform focused on prospecting. Its browser extensions help users fill their CRM with new prospects and their data directly from social media and company websites, providing enriched views with company information.

## Key Features
- **Browser extensions**: lead generation and data enrichment from social media and company websites
- **Enriched company views**: employee headcount, date founded, revenue
- **Contact filtering**: recently added, marked important, recently contacted
- **Workflow visualization**: visualize relationship progress with contacts
- **Workflow templates**: fundraising, job applicants, orders
- **Custom workflows**: create from scratch
- **Business card scanning**: AI-powered OCR reads and parses business cards to create or update contact records
- **AI email writing and outreach sequences**: AI assists with drafting outreach emails and optimizing sequence timing
- **AI email marketing add-on**: full campaign creation with AI-generated copy, dedicated list management, and segmentation
- **Lead scoring and follow-up prioritization**: AI surfaces which leads to act on first based on engagement signals
- **AI chat helper**: handles website visitor conversations when staff are unavailable; generates session summaries
