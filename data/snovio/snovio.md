## Overview

Snov.io is a sales automation and CRM platform focused on prospecting, email outreach, and lead generation for sales teams and recruiters.

## Key Features

- **Prospecting**: Lead search and contact database
- **Email Verification**: Verify email addresses before sending
- **Cold Outreach**: Email campaigns and drip sequences
- **CRM**: Contact and deal management
- **Email Finder**: Find professional email addresses
- **Domain Search**: Find emails by company domain
- **Integrations**: Native integrations plus Zapier connectivity

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: 50 credits, 100 recipients per day
- **Paid Plans**: Additional credits, recipients, and outreach features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).
