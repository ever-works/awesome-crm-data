## Overview

Tryton is an open-source enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform with customer relationship management functionality, first released in 2008. It provides a modular, high-level platform for business applications.

## Features

- Customer relationship management
- Accounting and financial management
- Inventory and stock management
- Sales and purchase management
- Project management
- Multi-language support (bg, ca, cs, de, en, es-ar, es-co, es-es, fr, nl, ru, sl)
- Web-based interface
- Cross-platform deployment
- Global search
- LDAP support
- Time tracking
- Email attachment to business objects

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPLv3 license.

## Deployment

Self-hosted; cross-platform deployment.

## Integrations

- PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite databases
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
