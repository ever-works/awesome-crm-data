## Overview

Dolibarr is an open-source ERP and CRM software suite first released in 2003. It is designed for small and medium-sized businesses, foundations, and freelancers to manage business activities including contacts, invoices, products, and stock.

## Features

- Customer relationship management
- Invoicing and billing
- Product and stock management
- Point of sale
- Electronic document management
- Project management
- Human resources management
- Agenda and calendar
- Multi-language support (over 30 languages)
- Web-based interface
- Google module for customer and agenda sync
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the GPLv3 license. SaaS hosting available from partners.

## Deployment

Self-hosted (cross-platform) or SaaS/cloud deployment.

## Integrations

- Google Calendar and Contacts
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
- Various modules via extension system
