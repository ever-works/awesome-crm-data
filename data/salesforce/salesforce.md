## Overview

Salesforce is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform founded by Marc Benioff in 1999. It provides sales, service, marketing, commerce, and analytics capabilities for organizations of all sizes.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Customer service and support
- Marketing automation
- Commerce cloud
- Analytics and reporting
- AI-powered insights (Einstein AI)
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (en, fi, fr, de, es, it, ja, ko, pt-br, ru, sv, th, zh-cn, zh-tw, da, nl-nl)
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking

## Pricing

Proprietary SaaS licensing model with tiered editions (Essentials, Professional, Enterprise, Unlimited). Pricing varies by edition and user count.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Office
- Email systems
- AppExchange marketplace with thousands of third-party integrations
- API for custom integrations

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
