## Overview

monday sales CRM is a customizable sales CRM platform designed for lead and pipeline management, contact tracking, and team collaboration.

## Key Features

- Lead Management
- Contact Management
- Pipeline Management
- Email Integration
- Customizable workflows
- Sales dashboards

## Pricing

Starts from $12 per seat per month. Free trial available.

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.

## Integrations

Native integrations with email, calendar, and productivity tools. API-based integrations available.
