## Overview

EngageBay is an all-in-one CRM platform designed for small businesses, combining sales, marketing, service, and live chat capabilities in a unified system.

## Key Features

- **Sales CRM**: Contact management, deal tracking, sales pipeline with Kanban view
- **Marketing**: Email marketing, landing pages, forms, email sequences
- **Service & Help Desk**: Ticketing, live chat, canned responses, AI chatbot
- **Unified Inbox**: All customer conversations (email, chat, social) in one place
- **Automation**: Workflow automation (paid plans)
- **Reporting**: Dashboards and reports for sales and marketing performance
- **Integrations**: Native integrations plus Zapier connectivity for thousands of apps
- **Mobile**: iOS and Android apps available

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: Up to 250 contacts, 1,000 branded emails per month, includes AI chatbot, ticketing, live chat, canned responses
- **Basic Plan**: $14.99/user/month
- **Growth Plan**: Higher tier with additional features
- **Pro Plan**: Enterprise-grade features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) with mobile apps for iOS and Android.
