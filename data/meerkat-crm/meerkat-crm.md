## Overview

Meerkat CRM is an open-source digital rolodex for personal relationship management. It features contact circles, relationship tracking, employment history, and birthday reminders.

## Features

- Contact circles
- Relationship tracking
- Employment history
- Birthday reminders
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/meerkatcrm/meerkat-crm
