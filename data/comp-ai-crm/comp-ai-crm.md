## Overview

Comp AI CRM is an open-source CRM operated by an AI agent. The agent researches contacts, schedules its own follow-ups, and records only sourced facts, reducing manual data entry.

## Features

- AI agent operation
- Contact research
- Automated follow-up scheduling
- Sourced fact recording
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/trycompai/crm
