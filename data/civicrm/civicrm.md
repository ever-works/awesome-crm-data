## Overview

CiviCRM is an open-source constituent relationship management system designed specifically for non-profit and advocacy organizations. First released in 2004, it integrates with popular content management systems including Drupal, WordPress, Joomla, and Backdrop.

## Features

- Constituent and donor management
- Membership management
- Event management and registration
- Email marketing and communications
- Fundraising and contribution tracking
- Case management
- Volunteer management
- Integration with Drupal, WordPress, Joomla, and Backdrop
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (over 40 languages)
- Time tracking via Agiliway's Time Tracker or CiviMobile
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPLv3 license. Hosted and support services available from partners.

## Deployment

Self-hosted; cross-platform deployment.
