## Overview

SuiteCRM is an open-source customer relationship management platform first released in 2013. It originated as a fork of SugarCRM when SugarCRM moved to a proprietary model, and provides a fully open-source alternative with similar functionality.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Marketing automation
- Customer support and case management
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (20 languages)
- Plugin-based Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking
- LDAP support
- Cross-platform deployment

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPLv3 license. Commercial support and services available from SuiteCRM Ltd and partners.

## Deployment

Self-hosted; cross-platform deployment.

## Integrations

- Office integration via plugin
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
- Various extensions via module loader
