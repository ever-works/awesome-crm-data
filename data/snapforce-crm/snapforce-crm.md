## Overview

Snapforce CRM is a customer relationship management platform first released in 2011. It provides sales force automation, contact management, and task management for small and medium-sized businesses.

## Features

- Contact and account management
- Sales pipeline tracking
- Task and activity management
- Email integration
- Web-based interface
- Cloud computing deployment
- Multi-language support (de, en, es, et, fi, fr, it, nl, pl, pt, tr, ko, ja, ru)
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Office integration
- Email clients
- Various third-party integrations

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
