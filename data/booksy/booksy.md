## Overview

Booksy is a business management and appointment scheduling platform designed for service-based businesses such as salons, spas, and wellness providers. It was first released in 2014 and helps businesses manage appointments, clients, and operations.

## Features

- Appointment scheduling and booking
- Client management
- Calendar and calendar sync
- Automated text and email marketing
- Staff schedule management
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Multi-language support (en, pl, es, pt, fr, de, uk, vi, zh)
- Web-based interface
- Time tracking
- Email attachment to business objects

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model. Pricing varies by plan and features.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Reserve with Google
- Email and SMS marketing
- Calendar synchronization
