## Overview

DaybydayCRM is an open-source business CRM that organizes leads, tasks, and clients around a team's daily workflow.

## Features

- Lead management
- Task management
- Client management
- Daily workflow organization
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM
