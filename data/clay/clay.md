## Overview

Clay is a personal CRM designed for individuals to manage their professional and personal relationships, automatically enriching contact data and surfacing updates about contacts.

## Key Features

- **Contact Management**: Organize and track personal and professional contacts
- **Automatic Updates**: Proactively notified about contacts' activities and changes
- **Data Enrichment**: Automatic contact information enrichment
- **Network Mapping**: Visualize relationships and connections
- **Reminders**: Follow-up and interaction reminders

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: 1,000 contacts
- **Paid Plans**: Additional contacts and features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) with web and mobile applications.
