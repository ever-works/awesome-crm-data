## Overview

Nametag is an open-source personal relationship manager that tracks birthdays, contact details, and maps connections between people. It helps users visualize and manage their personal network.

## Features

- Birthday tracking
- Contact detail management
- Relationship mapping
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/mattogodoy/nametag
