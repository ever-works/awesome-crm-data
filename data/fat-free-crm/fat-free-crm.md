## Overview

Fat Free CRM is an open-source Ruby on Rails CRM for managing contacts, leads, accounts, and opportunities.

## Features

- Contact management
- Lead management
- Account management
- Opportunity tracking
- Built on Ruby on Rails
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/fatfreecrm/fat_free_crm
