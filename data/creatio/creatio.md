## Overview

Creatio is a no-code platform for customer relationship management and business process automation. It supports sales, marketing, and service management workflows.

## Key Features

- Lead Management
- Sales Forecasting
- Opportunity Management
- Contact Management
- No-code process automation
- Pipeline management
- Email integration

## Pricing

Starts from $40 per user per month. No free plan available. Free trial available.

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.

## Integrations

Native integrations with email platforms and business applications. API-based integrations available.
