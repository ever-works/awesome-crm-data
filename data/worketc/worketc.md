## Overview

WORKetc is an all-in-one business management platform first released in 2009. It combines CRM, project management, billing, help desk, and time tracking functionality in a single system.

## Features

- Customer relationship management
- Project management
- Billing and invoicing
- Help desk and support
- Time tracking
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (de, en, es, fr, it, nl)
- Office integration (Outlook Addon and Google Docs)
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Outlook Addon
- Google Docs
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
