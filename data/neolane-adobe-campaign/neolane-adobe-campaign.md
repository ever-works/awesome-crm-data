## Overview

Neolane, now part of Adobe Campaign, is a proprietary marketing automation and campaign management platform first released in 2001. It enables organizations to manage cross-channel marketing campaigns, customer data, and delivery orchestration.

## Features

- Cross-channel campaign management
- Customer data management
- Email marketing
- Mobile marketing
- Direct mail management
- Campaign orchestration
- Audience segmentation
- Personalization

## Pricing

Proprietary or SaaS licensing model. Pricing available from Adobe sales.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) or on-premise deployment on Windows and Linux.

## Integrations

- Adobe Experience Cloud
- Email service providers
- Data management platforms

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
