## Overview

Django-CRM is an analytical CRM with tasks management, email marketing, and many more features. It is built for individual use, businesses of any size, or freelancers, and is designed to provide easy customization and quick development.

## Features

- Analytical CRM capabilities
- Tasks management
- Email marketing
- Easy customization
- Quick development
- Suitable for individual use, businesses, and freelancers
