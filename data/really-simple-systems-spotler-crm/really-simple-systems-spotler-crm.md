## Overview

Really Simple Systems, now Spotler CRM, is a customer relationship management platform first released in 2006. It provides sales and marketing automation functionality for small and medium-sized businesses.

## Features

- Contact and account management
- Sales pipeline tracking
- Email marketing
- Marketing automation
- Web-based interface
- Cloud computing deployment
- Single language support (en)
- Office integration

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Email marketing platforms
- Various third-party integrations

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
