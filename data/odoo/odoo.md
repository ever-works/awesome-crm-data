## Overview

Odoo is an open-source suite of business applications that includes CRM, sales, accounting, and inventory.

## Features

- CRM
- Sales management
- Accounting
- Inventory management
- Business applications suite
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/odoo/odoo
