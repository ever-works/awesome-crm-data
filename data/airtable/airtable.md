## Overview

Airtable is a cloud-based platform that combines the simplicity of a spreadsheet with the power of a database. It can be configured as a CRM using starter templates to track leads, contacts, and deals.

## Features

- Free plan supports up to five people per workspace
- Each base can hold up to 1,000 records on the free plan
- Multiple view types: grid, calendar, kanban, gallery, form
- Custom fields and rich field types (attachments, checkboxes, dropdowns, etc.)
- CRM starter templates available
- Automation capabilities for workflow triggers
- Integration with Zapier for connecting to other tools

## Pricing

- Free plan: up to 5 users, 1,000 records per base
- Paid plans start at $20/user/month (billed annually) for expanded records and advanced features

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS with web and mobile applications.
