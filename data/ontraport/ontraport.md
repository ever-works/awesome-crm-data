# Ontraport

## Overview
Ontraport is a CRM platform focused on automating online transactions. It helps businesses build infrastructure for sales journeys using Systems - sales assets strung together in sequences with templates for recapturing abandoned carts, lead magnets, and more.

## Key Features
- **Systems**: add sales assets and string them together in sequences
- **Templates**: recapturing abandoned carts, setting up lead magnets
- **Modular pages**: add pages with copy, input fields, and custom rules
- **Lead tracking**: keep track of lead progress through their journey
- **Campaign engagement history**: tracks actions taken, purchases, and memberships
- **Automation stats**: available from each automation to track performance
- **Payment and eCommerce platforms**: native integrations
- **File attachments**: attach case studies or other files to email threads
- **AI features**:
  - Workflow automation AI Assistant: segments contacts, drafts personalized emails, moderates comments, routes messages, analyzes call transcripts, generates content
  - Model and prompt controls: choose from multiple GPT models, configure system role, task prompt, temperature, Top P, credit limits
  - Conversation memory (AI Logs): stores prompts and responses for three months
  - MCP server: connects external AI clients (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor) to Ontraport data

## Pricing
From $99/month for 1 user; additional users at $19/user/month (for Plus plan; other plans have different pricing per extra user).

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS), with mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Integrations
- Zapier integration
- Native payment and eCommerce platform integrations
- MCP server for external AI clients
