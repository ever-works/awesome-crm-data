## Overview

Notion is an all-in-one workspace that combines notes, documents, databases, and project management tools. It can be adapted as a CRM using templates to track clients, projects, and relationships.

## Features

- Generous free plan for personal users with unlimited blocks
- Add unlimited contacts and data visualizations
- Up to 15 guests per page for collaboration without upgrading
- Customizable databases with multiple views (table, board, gallery, calendar, list)
- CRM templates available for quick setup
- Integration with Zapier for workflow automation

## Pricing

- Free plan available for personal users with generous limits
- Paid plans start at $10/user/month for additional features and guest limits

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS with web, desktop, and mobile applications.
