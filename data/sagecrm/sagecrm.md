## Overview

SageCRM is a customer relationship management platform by Sage Group, first released in 1981. It provides sales, marketing, and customer service functionality for small and medium-sized businesses.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Marketing automation
- Customer service and support
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Proprietary or SaaS licensing model. Pricing available from Sage sales.

## Deployment

On-premise deployment on Windows or SaaS/cloud deployment.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Office
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
- Sage accounting products

Proprietary software; no public repository.
