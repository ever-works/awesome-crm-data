## Overview

Act! CRM is a customer relationship management software originally created by Pat Sullivan and Mike Muhney in 1987. It is designed for small and medium-sized businesses to manage contacts, activities, and sales pipelines.

## Features

- Contact and activity management
- Sales pipeline tracking
- Email integration
- Mobile access (iOS, Android)
- Web-based and desktop deployment options
- MS SQL database backend
- Multi-language support (en, de, fr)
- LDAP support
- Web-based access
- Global search
- Time tracking
- Email attachment to business objects
