## Overview

Capsule CRM is a customer relationship management platform designed for small businesses, first released in 2009. It helps teams organize contacts, track sales pipelines, and manage tasks through a simple web interface and mobile apps.

## Features

- Contact and relationship management
- Sales pipeline and task tracking
- Email integration
- Gmail and G Suite integration
- Web-based interface
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Global search
- Email attachment to business objects
- Single language support (en)

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with various plan tiers based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Gmail and G Suite
- Email clients
