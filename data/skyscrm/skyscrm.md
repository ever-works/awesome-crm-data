# SkyScrm

## Overview
SkyScrm is a chat translation platform that provides high-quality, two-way real-time translation services for WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Facebook Messenger, and other instant messaging apps. It also offers multi-account support, allowing teams to manage multiple messaging accounts from one place.

## Key Features
- Two-way real-time translation across WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Facebook Messenger, and other instant messaging apps
- Multi-account support for managing several messaging accounts simultaneously
- CRM-aligned communication for customer conversations across channels

## Pricing
Paid with a free trial. $30/month for the port version (5 ports, starting monthly).

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).
