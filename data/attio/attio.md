# Attio

## Overview
Attio is an AI-native CRM built for startups. It offers a modern, flexible CRM platform with AI capabilities integrated throughout the product.

## Key Features
- **AI-native CRM**: built with AI capabilities at its core
- **Free plan**: available for getting started
- **Modern interface**: designed for startup teams
- **Flexible data model**: adapts to your processes

## Pricing
Free plan available; from $36/user/month.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).

## Integrations
- API available
- Zapier integration
