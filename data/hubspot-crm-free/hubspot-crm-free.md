## Overview

HubSpot CRM Free is a customer relationship management platform first released in 2014. It provides core CRM functionality including contact management, deal tracking, email integration, and task management at no cost, with paid upgrades for advanced features.

## Features

- Contact and company management
- Deal and task tracking
- Email integration (Gmail, G Suite, Outlook, Office 365)
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (en, fr, jp, de, pt, es)
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Office integration

## Pricing

Free plan available; freemium model with paid Marketing, Sales, Service, and CMS Hubs for advanced features.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Gmail and G Suite
- Outlook and Office 365
- HubSpot Marketing, Sales, and Service Hubs
- Various third-party integrations via App Marketplace
