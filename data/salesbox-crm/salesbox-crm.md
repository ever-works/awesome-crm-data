## Overview

Salesbox CRM is a customer relationship management platform first released in 2014. It provides sales pipeline management, contact management, and collaboration features for sales teams.

## Features

- Contact and account management
- Sales pipeline and deal tracking
- Team collaboration
- Web-based interface
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Multi-language support (de, en, es, fr, nl, pt, se, no, fi, tr, ro, ru, jp, ch, ko, hu)
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing based on features and users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Office integration
- Email clients
- Various third-party integrations

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
