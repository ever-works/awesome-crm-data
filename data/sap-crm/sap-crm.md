## Overview

SAP CRM is an enterprise customer relationship management platform by SAP SE, first released in 2000. It provides sales, marketing, service, and customer interaction management capabilities for large organizations.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Marketing management
- Customer service and support
- Interaction center
- Web channel management
- Analytics and reporting
- Multi-language support
- Web-based interface
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Proprietary licensing model. Enterprise pricing available from SAP sales.

## Deployment

On-premise deployment on Windows, Linux, and Unix.

## Integrations

- SAP HANA
- SAP ERP
- SAP Business Suite
- LDAP directory services

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
