## Overview

Clarify is an AI-native CRM designed to automate sales administrative work by connecting to email and calendar, handling meeting briefs, transcription, summaries, and CRM updates automatically.

## Key Features

- **AI Agent**: Automatically logs calls, emails, and next steps
- **Meeting Assistant**: Pre-call briefs, meeting transcription, post-meeting summaries
- **CRM Updates**: Automatically updates CRM with discussion points and next actions
- **Email & Calendar Sync**: Connects to email and calendar for data synchronization
- **Contact & Company Tracking**: Track people, companies, and deals
- **Basic Reporting**: Simple reporting and dashboards
- **Workflow Automation**: Flexible automation tools
- **Nudges**: Alerts when items need attention
- **Unlimited Seats**: No per-user pricing

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: Unlimited seats, capped at around 2,500 AI credits per month
- **Paid Plans**: Additional AI credits and features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) with web and Mac applications.
