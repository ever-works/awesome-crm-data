## Overview

Ever Gauzy is an open-source business management platform covering ERP, CRM, HR, applicant tracking, and projects.

## Features

- ERP
- CRM
- HR management
- Applicant tracking
- Project management
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.
