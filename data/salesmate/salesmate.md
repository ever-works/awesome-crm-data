# Salesmate

## Overview
Salesmate is a CRM platform focused on customization. It features a drag-and-drop interface and customizable modules that allow teams to adapt the CRM to their specific processes.

## Key Features
- **Drag-and-drop interface**: easy customization of the CRM layout
- **Customizable modules**: adapt the CRM to your business processes
- **Flexible structure**: CRM adapts to you, not the other way around

## Pricing
From $29/user/month.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).

## Integrations
- API available
- Zapier integration
