## Overview

SuperOffice CRM is a customer relationship management platform first released in 1990. It provides sales, marketing, and customer service functionality for businesses in Europe and globally.

## Features

- Contact and company management
- Sales pipeline and opportunity management
- Marketing campaigns
- Customer service and support
- Web-based interface
- Multi-language support (cn, de, dk, en, es, fi, fr, it, jp, nl, no, ru, se, zh)
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- Time tracking

## Pricing

Proprietary licensing model. Pricing available from SuperOffice sales.

## Deployment

On-premise deployment on Windows or cloud/SaaS options.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Office
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services
