## Overview

Folk is a CRM platform designed to help teams manage customer relationships end-to-end. The platform covers prospecting, lead engagement, phone conversations, pipeline management, and deal closing.

## Features

- **Prospect**: Find, enrich, and qualify contacts worth pursuing
- **Engage**: Interact with leads across channels and keep conversations personal at scale
- **Call**: Record phone conversations with your own number; prep, call, record, and review conversations directly in folk
- **Close**: Surface what to prioritize and next actions for the team to close more deals
- **Shared memory**: Design the ideal data model and feed it with context from 30+ integrations
- **AI agents**: Connect folk in your AI stack to let agents do groundwork
- **AI insights**: Explore data, uncover trends, and find answers with AI
