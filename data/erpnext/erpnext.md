## Overview

ERPNext is an open-source ERP suite that includes CRM, sales, accounting, inventory, and manufacturing modules.

## Features

- CRM
- Sales management
- Accounting
- Inventory management
- Manufacturing modules
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/frappe/erpnext
