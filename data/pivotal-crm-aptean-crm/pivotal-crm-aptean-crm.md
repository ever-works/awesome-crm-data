## Overview

Pivotal CRM, now marketed as Aptean CRM, is a customer relationship management platform first released in 1996. It provides sales, marketing, and customer service functionality for mid-sized and enterprise organizations.

## Features

- Sales force automation
- Marketing automation
- Customer service and support
- Web and Windows interface
- Multi-language support
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Proprietary licensing model. Pricing available from Aptean sales.

## Deployment

On-premise deployment on Windows.

## Integrations

- Microsoft Office
- Email systems
- LDAP directory services

## Repository

Proprietary software; no public repository.
