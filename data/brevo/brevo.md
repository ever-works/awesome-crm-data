## Overview

Brevo is a multichannel marketing and CRM platform that combines email marketing, CRM, SMS, WhatsApp, chat, and eCommerce capabilities in a single system.

## Key Features

- **CRM**: Contact management, deal tracking, sales pipeline
- **Email Marketing**: Campaigns, templates, automation, 300 free emails/day
- **SMS & WhatsApp**: Multichannel messaging
- **Chat**: Live chat and chatbot
- **eCommerce**: Online store integration, abandoned cart emails
- **Marketing Automation**: Workflow automation
- **Landing Pages**: Page builder and forms
- **Reporting**: Campaign analytics and dashboards
- **Integrations**: Native integrations plus Zapier connectivity

## Pricing

- **Free Plan**: 300 email sends/day, core CRM and email marketing features
- **Paid Plans**: Higher sending limits, advanced automation, and premium features

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).
