# Agile CRM

## Overview
Agile CRM is a modern customer relationship management platform that combines CRM with marketing automation, a social suite, and web analytics. It allows small businesses to sell and market like larger organizations, offering an all-in-one approach to customer engagement.

## Key Features
- Ease of use
- Cost-effective pricing
- Comprehensive feature set
- Marketing automation
- Social suite and web analytics
- Automation capabilities

## Pricing
Freemium with paid plans.

## Deployment
Cloud (SaaS).

## Integrations
- Third-party integrations
- API available
