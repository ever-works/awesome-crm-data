## Overview

Monica is an open-source personal relationship manager (PRM) for keeping track of friends, family, and business contacts. It helps users manage relationships, set reminders, and organize contact information.

## Features

- Contact management for friends, family, and business contacts
- Relationship tracking
- Reminders and follow-ups
- Self-hostable
- Open-source licensed under AGPL-3.0

## Pricing

Free and open-source under the AGPL-3.0 license.
