## Overview

Group-Office is an open-source groupware and CRM platform first released in 2003. It provides email, calendar, contacts, file management, and CRM functionality for teams and organizations.

## Features

- Customer relationship management
- Email and groupware
- Calendar and scheduling
- Contacts and address book
- File management and document sharing
- Project management
- Multi-language support (over 20 languages)
- Web-based interface
- Office integration
- Time tracking

## Pricing

AGPLv3 open-source edition available free; SaaS and proprietary editions available commercially.

## Deployment

Self-hosted (cross-platform) or SaaS/cloud deployment.

## Integrations

- Email systems
- Calendar systems
- LDAP directory services
