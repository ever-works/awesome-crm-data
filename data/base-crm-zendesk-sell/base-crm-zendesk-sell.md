## Overview

Base CRM, acquired by Zendesk and now marketed as Zendesk Sell, is a sales CRM platform launched in 2009. It helps sales teams manage leads, contacts, and deals through a web-based interface and mobile applications.

## Features

- Lead and contact management
- Sales pipeline and deal tracking
- Email integration
- Mobile apps for iOS, Android, and Windows Phone
- Multi-language support (de, en, es, fr, nl, pl, pt)
- Web-based interface
- Office integration
- Desktop environment support
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

SaaS subscription model. Pricing varies by plan and number of users.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Office integration
- Email clients
- LDAP directory services
