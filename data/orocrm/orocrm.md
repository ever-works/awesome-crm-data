## Overview

OroCRM is an open-source business CRM for marketing, sales, and customer service. Built on Symfony.

## Features

- Marketing management
- Sales management
- Customer service
- Built on Symfony
- Self-hostable

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted via GitHub repository: https://github.com/oroinc/crm-application
