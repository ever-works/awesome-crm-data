## Overview

Oracle Advertising and Customer Experience (CX) is an enterprise customer experience and CRM suite first released in 2011. It provides integrated marketing, sales, commerce, and service capabilities for large organizations.

## Features

- Marketing automation and campaign management
- Sales force automation
- Customer service and contact center
- Commerce and order management
- Customer data management
- Advertising and audience targeting
- Analytics and reporting
- Multi-language support (26 languages)
- Web-based interface
- Office integration
- Email attachment to business objects
- Global search
- LDAP support

## Pricing

Proprietary or SaaS licensing model. Enterprise pricing available from Oracle sales.

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS) deployment.

## Integrations

- Oracle Database
- Oracle Cloud Infrastructure
- Various enterprise systems via API

Proprietary software; no public repository.
